How to read this
Prediction number = a normalized signal score from −100 (strong short) to +100 (strong long), from each indicator's own math on the latest live bar — not a price target. Direction = the indicator's current call. Traffic light = backtested hit-rate: each indicator's causal (no-lookahead) signals are replayed across ~1000 historical bars; on every signal flip we check whether price moved in the signalled direction over the next N bars (your horizon). The light shows that hit-rate (🟢 ≥55% / 🟡 48–54% / 🔴 <48% / ⚫ <8 trades). 50% is the directional coin-flip baseline.

Honest caveats. This is a single-symbol, single-parameter, direction-only backtest with overlapping forward windows and no fees/slippage — it indicates relative reliability, not tradeable expectancy. Most popular indicators sit near 50%; that's the real, sobering result. Popularity ≠ profitability. Not financial advice. Data: Binance public market API.
