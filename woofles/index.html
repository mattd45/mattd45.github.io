A tiny stand with a very good queue

Woofles started in a home kitchen with one waffle iron and a dog who was extremely interested in what was happening. These days we load the cart into the van most weekends and set up wherever there are dogs to feed.

  • Small batches, made by hand — nothing mass produced.
  • Simple ingredients you could read out loud: oats, banana, egg, peanut butter.
  • No salt, no added sugar, no chocolate, no xylitol, no artificial nasties.
  • Compostable trays and paper bags, because fields should stay tidy.

Frequently woofed questions

Are they actually safe for dogs?

Yes. Every recipe is built from dog‑safe ingredients — oats, banana, egg and xylitol‑free peanut butter — with no salt, added sugar, chocolate, raisins or artificial sweeteners. They are treats though, so they should sit inside your dog’s daily treat allowance rather than replacing meals.

My dog has allergies. Can you work around them?

Usually, yes. Grain‑free and peanut‑free versions are available on custom orders. Everything is baked in a home kitchen that handles wheat, egg and peanuts, so we can’t promise zero cross‑contact — tell us about severe allergies and we’ll be straight with you about what we can do.

How long do they keep?

Five days in an airtight container, or up to a month in the freezer. Defrost at room temperature for an hour — no need to reheat.

Can I eat one?

You can, and people do. They’re unsalted and unsweetened, so expect “wholesome” rather than “brunch”.

Do you deliver?

We deliver locally for custom orders and offer collection at any event we’re trading at. Get in touch with your postcode and we’ll let you know.

Can you come to our event?

We’d love to. We’re fully self‑contained — cart, gazebo, power and public liability insurance — and we cover fetes, shows, markets and charity days. Send us the date, location and expected footfall.
