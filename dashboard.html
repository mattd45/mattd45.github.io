How to read this
Prediction number is a normalized signal score from −100 (strong short) to +100 (strong long), derived from each indicator's own math on live data — not a price target. Direction is the indicator's current call. The traffic light is a transparent confidence heuristic (signal strength, distance from threshold, trend agreement) bucketed green ≥65 / amber 40–64 / red <40 — it is not a calibrated probability of being right.

Not financial advice. Popularity of an indicator does not imply profitability. Indicators lag, repaint, and disagree by design. Data: Binance public market API. Built as a tool, use at your own risk.
